Inclusive by design

Accessibility statement

Nexora aims to provide a website that is usable by as many people as possible and targets WCAG 2.2 level AA.

What the site supports

The experience includes semantic structure, keyboard navigation, visible focus, text alternatives, responsive layouts, form labels and errors, and reduced-motion behavior.

Known limitations

This is a starter implementation. Complete manual keyboard, screen-reader, zoom, contrast, and content reviews before publishing it as a production website.

Report a problem

If you encounter a barrier, email accessibility@nexora.example and describe the page and issue.
