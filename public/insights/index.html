ERP readiness · 8 minute read

Do not start with the platform shortlist.

Replacement decisions often become technology decisions too early. A useful readiness review asks whether the organization can explain the operating problem, its value, the required changes, and who will own the result.

Can you state the business case without module names?

Describe the decisions, lead times, costs, controls, customer outcomes, or risks that must improve. If the case only lists technical obsolescence and product features, it is incomplete.

Which operating differences are truly necessary?

Separate variations that create competitive or regulatory value from those inherited through history. This distinction shapes the global template, governance, data, and adoption effort.

Is the data problem owned?

Migration does not fix unclear definitions, weak stewardship, duplicate masters, or disconnected planning assumptions. Put durable ownership in place before design decisions depend on the data.

Who owns improvement after go-live?

An ERP is an operating product. Assign business and technology ownership, measures, decision rights, and an improvement rhythm that survives the program.
